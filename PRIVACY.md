# Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026.

What the Tetris does not create accounts, show ads, use analytics, or send gameplay data to a server. The app stores best scores and best levels locally on the player's device using platform preferences.

The locally stored game data contains no name, email address, precise location, advertising identifier, or other account information. It can be removed by clearing the site's/app's storage or uninstalling the app.

This policy must be reviewed before adding crash reporting, analytics, cloud saves, leaderboards, advertising, purchases, or any other networked service.

Questions can be submitted through the project's [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/allingaming/whatthetetris/issues).
